# Product Vision

CoreHa helps restaurants protect the reliability of their kitchen equipment and make safer equipment investments. The product combines full-service maintenance oversight, expert equipment guidance, and a simplified supplier-quote process that makes purchasing decisions clearer and more technical.

## Initial audience

Restaurant owners and managers responsible for kitchen equipment and operational continuity. The first release focuses on restaurant workflows and real operational pain points in food service environments.

## Customer problem

Restaurants often struggle with equipment upkeep, missed maintenance windows, and unclear buying decisions when replacing or upgrading machinery. In many cases the restaurant does not have a reliable maintenance cycle, and the purchase process is based more on guesswork than technical fit.

A restaurant may not know which equipment needs maintenance, when it is critical, or which technical requirements are essential for a correct replacement or upgrade. Without the right guidance, poor purchasing decisions can lead to higher costs, downtime, and operational inefficiency.

## Product approach

- Maintain a digital inventory of critical equipment after on-site assessment.
- Trigger high-priority alerts when preventive maintenance is due.
- Let restaurant users confirm service need and schedule action quickly.
- Offer direct expert consultation when the restaurant needs a new appliance or replacement.
- Capture technical requirements through a short structured form or after a phone call.
- Generate supplier quote requests and centralize supplier replies.
- Prepare a clear technical and commercial comparison for the client using admin expertise and AI assistance.

## Value

- Fewer missed maintenance events and less operational downtime.
- Clearer, higher-priority alerts for urgent service needs.
- More accurate equipment recommendations based on kitchen constraints and technical requirements.
- Faster supplier request generation and better structured offer analysis.
- A premium consulting model that combines operational service, technical guidance, and supplier intermediation.
